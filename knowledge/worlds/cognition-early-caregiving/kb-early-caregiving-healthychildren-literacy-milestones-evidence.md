## Fundamentals

**Thesis:** Early literacy is **staged interaction with books and sound**, not early formal reading. Prenatal reading = bonding optional; real joint engagement ramps with motor control.

| Age band | Milestones (HealthyChildren) | Parent role |
|----------|-------------------------------|-------------|
| **~3 months** | Babble, imitate sounds, smile at voice; swipe at books | Games, face-to-face sound play |
| **12–18 months** | Sit unsupported; carry books; point «where is…»; notice upside-down book | Hand board books; answer pointing |
| **18–24 months** | Turn pages; name pictures; **finish familiar sentence** when parent pauses; recite to dolls | Pause in favorite books — child fills slot |

### Implications

- **Pause before completing sentence** = natural cloze / turn-taking — not multiple-choice exam.
- Child as **storyteller to toys** — private speech precursor; don't interrupt for grammar.
- Upside-down book recognition = orientation skill — respect stage.
