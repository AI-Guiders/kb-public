## Fundamentals

**Thesis:** Language begins at **birth** — connection motive («babies want to connect with you»). Wide range for first words (9–18 months); track **progression** of sounds, gestures, facial expressions.

### Five practices (synthesized across ZTT resources)

1. **Treat baby as conversational partner** — eye contact, coo back, repeat gurgles.
2. **Respond to gestures** — arms up → «you want to be picked up»; validates communication.
3. **Pause** — ask questions even to preverbal infants; wait for kick, reach, look.
4. **Narrate daily routines** — link words to objects/actions in real time («socks in the tall dresser»).
5. **Quiet time** — no constant TV/radio; space for babble and play.

### Older toddler extensions

- **Open-ended questions** — «What do you think about rainy weather?»
- **Build on language** — expand pretend play with descriptive talk.
- **Celebrate every communication attempt** — effort over correctness.

### Parentese

Melodic, higher-pitched speech with elongated vowels — **distinct from** random baby talk; supports sound-to-meaning mapping (ZTT resource on infant sounds).
