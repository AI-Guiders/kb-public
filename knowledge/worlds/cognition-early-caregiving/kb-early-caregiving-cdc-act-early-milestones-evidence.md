## Fundamentals

**Thesis:** Milestones = skills **≥75%** children reach by age — **monitoring tool**, not daily test. Parent tip on 15m page: early words are **approximations**; adult **repeats and expands** («ba» → «Ball, yes, that's a ball»).

### Language/communication — 15 months

- Tries 1–2 words besides mama/dada (e.g. «ba» for ball)
- Looks at familiar object when named
- Follows directions with gesture + words
- Points to request or get help

### Language/communication — 24 months (2 years)

- Points to things in book when asked
- Says at least **two words together** («more milk»)
- Points to ≥2 body parts when asked
- More gestures than wave/point (kiss, nod yes)

### Parent actions (CDC «What you can do»)

- Help child speak — **repeat and add** to approximations
- Act early if regression or persistent «not yet» — talk to doctor, ask about **developmental screening**
