## Fundamentals

**Thesis:** Early language grows in **relational health** — back-and-forth vocalizing and gesturing between child and caring adult (**serve and return**). Play in everyday moments (diaper, feeding, silly faces) is learning.

### Key messages (AAP timeline excerpts)

- **Serve and return:** child babbles/gestures → adult responds with same modality (vocalize, gesture) — builds brain foundation.
- **Play:** developmentally appropriate play with parents promotes language, self-regulation, executive function.
- **Reading:** regular shared reading stimulates brain patterns and parent-child bond; **never too early**; point to colors/shapes/characters.
- **2 months+:** happiness at parent's approach; smile when talked to.

### What to say / what to know pattern

AAP frames each age as **What to Know** (mechanism) + **What to Say** (parent-facing script ideas) — scripts are **examples**, not fixed utterance hubs.
