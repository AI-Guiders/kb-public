## Fundamentals

**Thesis:** Responsive **back-and-forth** between adult and young child builds neural architecture for learning. Language acquisition **requires social interaction** to instigate learning (HCDC cites Kuhl 2007; contingent response to babbling — Goldstein et al.).

### Five steps

| Step | Adult action | Why (HCDC) |
|------|--------------|------------|
| 1. **Share focus** | Notice serve — look, point, sound, muscle tension | Joint attention |
| 2. **Support & encourage** | Return with smile, nod, comfort, help, «I see!» | Child knows communication works |
| 3. **Name it** | Label what child sees/does/feels | Language connections before speech |
| 4. **Take turns & wait** | Pause for child's response; volley short or long | Self-control, confidence, time to form reply |
| 5. **Endings & beginnings** | Notice «all done», walk away, new toy — follow or redirect gently | Transitions; child can lead sometimes |

### Practice notes

- **Don't jump in too soon** — babies need longer response latency (First Things First, aligned with step 4).
- **Parentese** (higher pitch, stretched vowels) — attention, not «dumb down».
- **Home language** — more rich input in comfortable language wins.
